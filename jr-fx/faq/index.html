JR FX / HELP

A little help.

Quick answers for the launch candidate. For step-by-step instructions, open the manual.

Does JR FX make beats or host instruments?

JR FX is an audio effect and performance box. It processes a loop, file, live input or host audio. It does not replace a sampler or host other AUv3 instruments.

Can I use it with another music app?

Yes: use a compatible AUv3 host to route another music app into JR FX, or import an exported audio file into standalone JR FX. Check the host routing and transport.

Why is there no sound?

Check SOURCE, PLAY/host transport, the physical audio route, VOLUME and any enabled stages. For INPUT, check microphone permission, channel pair and monitoring. Turn off feedback-heavy effects while checking routing.

Follow the sound checks

Why are my effects off on launch?

Standalone starts safely with effect stages off. Select a category, then enable its power. Opening SEQ or selecting an effect name does not switch power on.

Are EDIT 1/2 the same as BUS A/B?

No. EDIT 1/2 are sequencer patterns. BUS A/B are audio effect paths. PLAY chooses which sequencer pattern runs independently of the edit selection.

Can I process a whole song?

Yes. Import a supported file and use offline WAV export. Long files are streamed/processed in bounded chunks. Loading time and free storage still depend on the file and device.

Why does export sound different from my live performance?

Offline export uses current settings, not a recording of previous gestures, and has a two-second tail. Use REC AUDIO to capture a live performance and longer tails.

Are the sampler models exact hardware clones?

They are independent hardware-inspired models. Known formats and published behavior are distinguished from DSP approximations in MODEL INFO. No original manufacturer firmware is included.

Is there a subscription or feature unlock?

No. The planned App Store release is one paid purchase with all released features, AUv3 and included skins. Launch pricing is US$2.99 for the first month after release, then US$4.99. Local prices are set by the App Store.

How do I change skins?

Open SETUP → INFO → SKIN. Modern BK is the default; your saved choice stays selected. Appearance only — your sound and controls stay the same. Modern BK: Dark hardware. Smooth lettering. JR XL: Warm ivory. Bold pixels. JR BK: Charcoal with ivory pixel keys. JR BL: Deep blue with a lime display.

Where are recordings, and can I record in the background?

Use REC → TAKES to preview, rename and share. Leaving the app ends/finalizes a take. Background live-input audio and background recording are different behaviors.
