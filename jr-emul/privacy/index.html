JR EMUL / APP PRIVACY

Your audio stays in your workflow.

JR EMUL processes audio on your device. Microphone permission is used when you select live input. Imported audio, recordings, exported takes and settings are stored locally by the app. System file providers and share destinations operate under their own permissions and privacy terms.

The current app implementation does not include an account requirement, advertising SDK or app analytics service. Files are shared when you choose a system share or export destination. Your AUv3 host may store plug-in state in its own projects.

Website Studio Notes signup is separate from app audio processing. Contact jazzyrhythmer@gmail.com for privacy questions. Last updated November 20, 2026.
