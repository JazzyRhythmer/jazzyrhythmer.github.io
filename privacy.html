Privacy, simply.

This site is operated by Jazzy Rhythmer. For questions about your subscription or to request deletion of your information, contact jazzyrhythmer@gmail.com.

Studio Notes

If you subscribe, your email address is sent to Kit, the email service used to manage Studio Notes. We use it to send the music, studio sessions, and occasional sounds and software updates you requested. You must confirm your email before joining the mailing list.

Kit also processes information associated with signup and email delivery, such as technical request details, subscription status, and email interactions. This website uses Kit's embedded signup script, so your browser connects to Kit when loading the form. Kit is an overseas service provider; its processing and international data-transfer practices are described in Kit's privacy policy.

Your subscription is managed through Kit. Use the unsubscribe link in an email to stop future updates, or contact us to request deletion. Suppression and subscription records may be retained to respect opt-out requests and manage delivery. Subscribing is optional; you can browse this website without joining.

Website and external services

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Hosting and linked services may process technical request information. We have not added advertising pixels or independent website analytics. Listening, video playback, support, and purchases take place on the linked services under their own privacy practices. Payment details are not collected on this website.
