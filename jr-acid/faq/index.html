JR ACID / HELP

A little help.

Quick answers for the launch candidate. For step-by-step instructions, open the manual.

Can I export a WAV directly from standalone?

This version exports MIDI and portable .jracid sessions. To record or render the synth audio, load JR ACID as an AUv3 instrument in a compatible host and use that host’s recording/render controls. MIDI files do not contain audio.

Follow the save and export steps

Is JR ACID a synthesizer or a MIDI sequencer?

Both. It has its own acid voice and can also send supported MIDI notes, clock and controls. Use local sound off when you only want to drive external gear.

What is the difference between GENERATE and MUTATE?

GENERATE creates a new phrase from the recipe and locks. MUTATE changes eligible unlocked material probabilistically, so a low setting may produce no audible change.

Do locks stop me editing a note?

No. Locks constrain generation and mutation. Manual edits and full-patch operations remain available.

Why did a preset replace my pattern?

Choose SOUND ONLY, PATTERN ONLY or BOTH in PRESETS before loading. Full patch operations can intentionally replace both.

How long can a pattern be?

Up to64 steps, with page navigation, FOLLOW, duplication and phrase-editing tools.

Why is there no sound in an AUv3 host?

Load JR ACID as an instrument, route MIDI to it, route its audio to an output, and check the host transport or incoming MIDI. Confirm local sound is enabled when you want the internal voice.

Follow the sound checks

How do I control a hardware synth?

Choose MIDI port/channel, an articulation mode and NOTES/CLOCK routing. Avoid feedback loops. Physical USB/DIN/Bluetooth behavior depends on the interface and device.

Are skins sound presets?

No. Modern and JR skins change the interface only. Use PRESETS and the sound controls to change the instrument.

Is there a subscription or feature unlock?

No. The planned App Store release is one paid purchase with released features and AUv3 included. Launch pricing is US$4.99 for the first month, then US$9.99. Local App Store prices vary.

How do I change skins?

Tap SKIN in the top bar. Modern BK is the default; your saved choice stays selected. Appearance only — your sound and controls stay the same. Modern BK: Black panel. Lime accents. Modern: Graphite hardware with smooth type. Modern Light: Light grey with a soft metal feel. Modern Yellow: A warm yellow hardware panel. Modern Red: Deep red with lime highlights. Modern Purple: Muted purple with lime highlights. JR: Ivory pixels and a green LCD. JR 303: Vintage ivory pixels. Amber LCD.

Can I recover a project?

Try the explicit CHECKPOINT/RECOVER workflow and preserve your original files. A checkpoint is not a guarantee that every unsaved action survives an interruption.
