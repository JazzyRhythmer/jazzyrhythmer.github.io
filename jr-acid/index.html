THE WORKFLOW

Generate. Lock.
Make it yours.

A focused instrument surface, with room for the details when you need them. Modern and JR skins share the same controls and sound.

01 / GENERATE

Find the next line.

Choose root, scale and density. Generate a phrase or mutate the unlocked part of one you already like.

02 / SEQUENCE

Get inside the pattern.

Up to 64 steps, note editing, accent, slide and independent generation locks. Record from the on-screen keys.

03 / SYNTH

Shape the acid voice.

Oscillators, 303-style diode voice or LADDER 24, drive, glide and six performance knobs.

04 / FX RACK

Build the finish.

Delay, reverb, parallel compression, phaser and output shaping. Keep each module active or bypassed.

05 / MODULATION

Keep it moving.

LFO controls and tempo-aware movement, with dedicated modulation pages and sequenced DUCK.

06 / MIDI + AUv3

Play with the rest of your setup.

Use it as an AUv3 instrument, send MIDI notes/clock to other gear, and save sounds, patterns and projects.

A CLOSER LOOK

See where the sound goes.

Swipe or scroll through actual iPad screens. Tap an image to inspect it.

CHOOSE YOUR SURFACE

Same sound. Your style.

Tap SKIN in the top bar. Modern BK is the default; your saved choice stays selected. Appearance only — your sound and controls stay the same.
