JR ACID / PRIVACY

Your music stays yours.

Effective November 18, 2026. This policy covers the JR ACID iPhone/iPad app and its AUv3 extension.

App data

The app does not require an account and does not include developer-operated advertising, analytics or tracking. We do not receive your audio, MIDI, projects or presets through the app.

Files and audio

Audio, recordings, presets, settings and sessions are processed or stored on your device, or in a location you choose through Apple Files or your host. Importing a cloud-hosted file may involve that storage provider. In AUv3, your host manages its own project and audio data.

Permissions

MIDI device access is used for the musical connections you choose. Basic internal-synth operation does not require a microphone or an account.

Sharing and support

Export and sharing happen only through the destinations you choose. If you email support, we receive the message and any files you send, use them to respond, and do not add you to marketing email without a separate signup. Avoid sending private recordings or unnecessary personal information.

Apple and other services

Apple handles App Store purchases and may provide platform diagnostics according to your system choices and its privacy policy. Hosts, cloud storage and sharing destinations have their own policies. This product website uses a separate optional Studio Notes email signup; see the website privacy policy.

Your choices

You can revoke permissions in Settings, delete local files and app data, choose what you share, or contact us about support correspondence. Back up music you want to keep before deleting it.
